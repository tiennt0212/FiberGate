# Reminder Sections

Reminders are short-form, lookup-oriented entries. No extended explanation or WHY — just what to remember.
For the WHY and principle behind a pattern, see `best-practices/`.

---

## Section Types

### api-change
Deprecated or renamed APIs between major versions. Format: old → new table.

### gotcha
Silent failures, unexpected behaviors, or counter-intuitive defaults.

### convention
Project or team conventions that don't need justification — just consistency.

### file-location
Where things live in this project (imports, config files, entry points).
